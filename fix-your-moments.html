Our story

Fix Your Moments began with one camera and a habit Koushik Pramanik could never shake: noticing the moment just before everyone else did.

For years photography was his passion, pursued at family weddings, festivals and on long walks through the city. In 2022 he turned that passion into a profession and founded Fix Your Moments, with a simple promise to every client: the day is yours, and the photographs should feel like it.

"I don't want you to remember posing. I want you to remember being there."

Today the studio covers weddings and pre-wedding shoots, haldi and rice ceremonies, corporate events and editorial portraits. The approach hasn't changed: listen first, plan carefully, then disappear into the crowd and let the day happen.
